# Support — Orch Neural Network

Orch Neural Network runs a real GPT-2 language model on your iPhone or iPad and visualises every step of next-word prediction.

**Questions, bugs, or feature requests:** email wonmor@yahoo.com, or open an issue on this repository.

**Frequently asked**

- *Why are the suggestions odd sometimes?* The model is DistilGPT2, trained on 2019 web text. It is a learning tool, not a production keyboard.
- *Does anything leave my device?* No. The app has no network access. See PRIVACY.md.
- *Why can't I see all 82 million weights at once?* A 4K screen has about 8 million pixels. The Weights tab shows each matrix one at a time, sampled to fit.

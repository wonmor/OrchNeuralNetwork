# Privacy Policy — Inside LLM (formerly Orch Neural Network)

_Last updated: November 8, 2026_

Inside LLM is an educational app for iPhone, iPad and Android that runs a small language model on your device to show how next-word prediction works.

**We do not collect any data.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. Text you type is processed only in the app's memory on your device to compute predictions, and is discarded when you leave the screen. Nothing you type is transmitted, stored on servers, or shared with anyone.

**Optional tips.** The app contains an optional tip jar. If you open it, the app asks the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) for the tip prices, and if you choose to leave a tip, the payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details, and a tip unlocks nothing. This is the app's only network use.

The app does not use cookies, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

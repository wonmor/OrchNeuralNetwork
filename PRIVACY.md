# Privacy Policy — Orch Neural Network

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

Orch Neural Network is an educational app that runs a small language model on your device to show how next-word prediction works.

**We do not collect any data.** The app has no network access, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. Text you type is processed only in the app's memory on your device to compute predictions, and is discarded when you leave the screen. Nothing is transmitted, stored on servers, or shared with anyone.

The app does not use cookies, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

If you have questions about this policy, contact: wonmor@yahoo.com

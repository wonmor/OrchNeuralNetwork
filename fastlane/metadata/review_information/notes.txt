Orch Neural Network is an educational, fully offline visualiser of a small open-source language model (DistilGPT2, Apache 2.0). The app contains its own transformer inference engine written from scratch in Swift/Accelerate (no Core ML, no third-party SDKs, no network access) and shows attention heatmaps, neuron activations, the logit lens and every weight matrix as the model runs. It shares no code, assets or design with other apps on this account; build 2 has a new interface and icon. No sign-in is needed. Suggested test: tap the "I am…" sample button on the Predict tab, then open the Inside tab and tap the play button, then open Weights and tap any matrix.
